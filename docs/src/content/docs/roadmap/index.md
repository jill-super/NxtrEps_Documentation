The master workbook is the program view; the TRM one-pagers are the technology views.
Read this page first, then dive into [TRM index](../trm/) for detail.

## What the workbook is

- **25 sheets**: per-technology scoring + timing + applicability roll-ups to November 2016.
- **Scoring rubric** (shared strings in the workbook): cost-per-piece bands
  (`<$0.25 … >$3.00`), mass bands (`<50 g … >800 g`), packaging improvement,
  performance (new function / closes gap / quoting value), customer reach
  (one → most → regulation), quality (FTQ, PPM, warranty, error-proofing),
  value-stream risk (supplier, FX, logistics).

:::tip[How to read a TRM against the roadmap]
Match the one-pager's **Motivation (score)** and **Timing** against the workbook's
scoring columns and milestone rows. Applicability (`one / some / most customers`,
`required by regulation`) tells you whether a technology is niche or platform.
:::
