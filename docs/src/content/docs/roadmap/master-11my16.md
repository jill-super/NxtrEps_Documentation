## At a glance

- **25 worksheets** covering cost, mass, packaging, performance, customer reach,
  quality and value-stream risk plus per-technology timing.
- Cost bands: `<$0.25 / <$1.00 / <$2.00 / <$3.00 / >$3.00` per piece.
- Mass bands: `<50 g / <200 g / <500 g / <800 g / >800 g`.
- Packaging: from “fits almost any packaging” to “<5% critical-dimension gain”.
- Performance: new function/capability, closes competitive gap, quoting value.
- Reach: one → some → most customers, or regulation-driven.
- Quality: FTQ +1%, IPTV@36MIS −3, error-proofing product-vs-process.

## How to use it

1. **Filter by reach + timing** to find what lands on your program (C1xx, K2xx, PSA…).
2. **Sort by cost/mass deltas** for disruptive candidates (LO CEPS, plastic housing/pulley).
3. **Cross-check safety items** (SE02/SE07/SE14, LoA taxonomy) against regulation rows.
4. **Open the TRM deck** linked from [TRM index](../trm/) for the narrative behind any row.
