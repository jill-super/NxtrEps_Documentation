## Key points

- Protection-coverage trade must be checked

## Slide-text preview

> (1 slides) Remove Reverse-Battery Protection FET from EPS Control Modules | Description | Remove the reverse-battery protection FET from the EPS control module, and replace with a short circuit | Add local reverse-battery protection (diodes) at power supply and gate drive | For a reverse-battery condition, depend on the vehicle fuse to blow before an inverter FET is damaged | Risk: Inverter FET Damage | Fuses have huge variation in performance | An MTA MIDI fuse at 200% of current rating is guaranteed to blow somewhere between 3s and 100s | An MTA MIDI fuse at 500% of current rating is guaranteed to blow somewhere between 0.1s and 1.0s | Testing has shown that we can not depend on equal current sharing among the 3 legs of the inverter | 1 | Status | We are preparing to run PSA RA04 test on PSA BMPV controller | (I.
